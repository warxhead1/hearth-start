Hi. Let's get you into hearth.

Six steps, about ten minutes, no passwords. Hearth lives on Eric's computer, which is shy: it only talks to phones inside a private tunnel called Tailscale. This page walks you through the tunnel and in the front door.

Checking whether your phone can see hearth...
  1. 1

    Get the Tailscale app

    It's free, it's from a company called Tailscale Inc., and it's the tunnel.

    Open the App Store

    Tap Get, then come back to this page.

  2. 2

    Sign in to Tailscale

    1. Open Tailscale and tap Get Started (or Log in).
    2. Choose Sign in with Google and pick your Gmail account.
    3. When iPhone asks to Add VPN Configurations, tap Allow and confirm with Face ID or your passcode. That's the tunnel, not anything sneaky.
    4. Make sure the big switch at the top of the Tailscale app is on (it says Connected).
    Wait, what is this thing?

    Tailscale makes a private network that only your phone and the machines you're allowed into can see. Eric shared exactly one machine with you: hearth. Your other internet traffic does not go through it, and a shared machine can only answer your phone, never reach into it.

  3. 3

    Accept the hearth share

    Accept hearth

    It opens a Tailscale page in Safari. Sign in with Google again, with the same account as step 2, and tap Accept.

    Open the email from Tailscale that says Eric shared a device with you, and tap the button in it. Sign in with Google using the same account as step 2, then tap Accept.

    Got a second email about a machine called studio? You can ignore that one; you reach the studio from inside hearth.

  4. 4

    Open hearth

    Open hearth

    Open this page from the link Eric texted you; it carries hearth's address.

    If Safari says it can't find the server, go back to Tailscale, check the switch is on, and try again. The dot at the top of this page turns green when your phone can see hearth.

  5. 5

    Make your passkey

    On hearth, tap Make my passkey and look at your phone. Face ID, done. From now on Face ID is your login; there is nothing to remember and nothing to type.

    If iPhone asks where to save it, pick iCloud Keychain (the default).

  6. 6

    Put hearth on your Home Screen

    1. In Safari, with hearth open, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    3. From now on, open hearth from that icon. iPhone only delivers hearth's notifications to the icon version.

You're in.

Hearth is on your Home Screen and Face ID is your key. Go poke at the ventures.

Stuck? You don't have to explain: Eric's hearth notices which step you're on and nudges him. Texting him "step 4 is being weird" also works.
