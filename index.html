Hi. Let's get you into hearth.

Four steps, about five minutes, no passwords. Ever. Hearth lives on Eric's computer, which is shy: it only talks to phones inside his private network, called Tailscale. Once your phone is in, hearth already knows it's you.

Checking whether your phone can see hearth...
  1. 1

    Get the Tailscale app

    It's free, it's from a company called Tailscale Inc., and it's the private network.

    Open the App Store

    Tap Get, then come back to this page.

  2. 2

    Accept the Tailscale invite

    Accept the Tailscale invite

    It opens a Tailscale page in Safari. Choose Sign in with Google, pick your Gmail account, and accept.

    Open the email from Tailscale inviting you to join Eric's network and tap the button in it. Choose Sign in with Google, pick your Gmail account, and accept.

    Then, in the Tailscale app:

    1. Log in with Google using that same account. Already logged in to your own network? Tap your account at the top, then Add account, and log in again.
    2. When it asks which network, pick Eric's, not your own.
    3. If iPhone asks to Add VPN Configurations, tap Allow. That's the private network, nothing sneaky.
    4. The big switch at the top should be on (it says Connected).
    Wait, what is this thing?

    Tailscale is a private network between Eric's machines and now your phone. Only your hearth traffic uses it; the rest of your internet goes the normal way. Your own Tailscale network stays yours. The app switches between the two, one at a time.

  3. 3

    Open hearth

    Open hearth

    Open this page from the link Eric texted you. It has hearth's address in it.

    That's the whole login. Hearth sees which Tailscale account your phone is on and signs you in by itself. Nothing to type, nothing to remember.

    If Safari says it can't find the server, go back to Tailscale, check you're on Eric's network with the switch on, and try again. The dot at the top of this page turns green when your phone can see hearth.

  4. 4

    Put hearth on your Home Screen

    1. In Safari, with hearth open, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
    3. From now on, open hearth from that icon. iPhone only delivers hearth's notifications to the icon version.

You're in.

Hearth is on your Home Screen, and it knows you by your phone. Go poke at the ventures.

Stuck? You don't have to explain. Eric's hearth notices which step you're on and nudges him. Texting him "step 3 is being weird" also works.

This page holds no data. Your name and hearth's address come from the link Eric sent you and never leave your phone.
